The idea

Adventure should give you reasons to come home.

DK's Adventures is not a kitchen-sink modpack, a factory progression pack, or a quest book with a finish line. Its core loop is simple: prepare at home, leave on an expedition, discover something worth keeping, return, improve, and decide where to go next.

Prepare→ Explore→ Discover→ Return→ Improve

Built around four pillars

More Minecraft, not more chores.

01

Explore

Large terrain, distant horizons, rare caves, reworked structures, and destinations with room for wilderness between them.

Tectonic · Alex's Caves · Distant Horizons · YUNG's structures

02

Grow through gear

Progress through equipment, materials, artifacts, and discoveries instead of character levels or stat-grinding skill trees.

Tetra · Better Combat · Artifacts

03

Build a real home

Your kitchen, smithy, warehouse, workshop, maps, and machines are part of the adventure rather than a menu you eventually replace.

Create · Farmer's Delight · Supplementaries · Quark

04

Take your time

Short sessions still matter. Advancements provide optional ideas, travel becomes easier only after discovery, and death is recoverable.

BACAP · Map Atlases · Waystones · Comforts

The experience

Designed for one world worth keeping.

No mandatory megafarms

Vanilla farms and automation remain available, but normal progression should not force you to optimize the fun out of the world.

Optional engineering

Create adds physical workshop machinery and base projects. It is convenience and creativity, not a required technology ladder.

Preconfigured from the start

Mods, datapacks, shaders, backups, performance settings, travel rules, and balance decisions are maintained as part of the pack.

Built to stay stable

World generation can evolve during 0.x testing. After 1.0, save compatibility and the permanent world take priority.

In development

DK's Adventures 0.1.0-dev

The core performance, world-generation, exploration, equipment, base-building, storage, travel, recovery, and shader layers are assembled. Creature, atmosphere, compatibility, and final balance passes are still in progress.

Do not start the permanent world from this development build yet.
